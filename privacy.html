Privacy Policy

Updated October 23, 2026

Blinkplate is a meal tracker operated by Emil Ibrishimov. This policy covers the app and this website.

What we collect

  • Your tracking data: meals, foods, recipes, quantities, meal times and time zones, nutrient preferences and targets, and profile details you enter, including sex, birth year, height, weight, activity level, and goal.
  • Photos and AI records: meal and package photos you submit, barcodes, relevant meal history, AI inputs and results, and your corrections.
  • Technical information: an automatically assigned account identifier, app and catalog versions, request times, IP addresses and service logs. If you send feedback or contact us, we receive what you share; TestFlight may also provide crash reports and testing feedback.

How we use and share it

We use this data to run the app, calculate nutrient targets, recognize food, troubleshoot problems, prevent abuse, and improve Blinkplate and its recognition accuracy. This can include reviewing submitted photos, AI results, and corrections.

We use Supabase to store app data and photos on servers in the United States. For photo recognition and package-label reading, we send photos and relevant food and meal context to DeepInfra, our AI processing provider.

We may disclose data when legally required. This website’s hosting provider may process IP addresses and request logs to serve and secure it.

Retention and your choices

We retain uploaded app data, photos, and AI records to operate and improve Blinkplate, including records of items you delete in the app. Deleting an item or uninstalling the app does not erase those server records.

You can edit your tracking data and control camera access in your device settings. To request deletion of your server data or withdraw consent to its use, email contact@blinkplate.com. We will need to identify your automatically created account; please contact us before uninstalling the app.

Contact

For privacy questions, email contact@blinkplate.com.
